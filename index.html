Our Story

Good food brings people together.

House of Pizza started with one simple idea: make pizza the way we believe it should be made — patiently, generously and with the best ingredients we can find.

Our dough rests for hours, our sauces are made from scratch, and every pizza is finished by hand before it enters our blazing-hot oven.
